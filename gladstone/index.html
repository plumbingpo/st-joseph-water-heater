Getting More Years From Your Water Heater in Gladstone

The best way to avoid a surprise leak is simple maintenance. We recommend that homeowners flush the tank once a year (more often with hard water), have the anode rod checked every three to five years, test the relief valve annually to make sure it isn't stuck, and keep the thermostat around 120 degrees, which reduces the risk of scalding and cuts down on scale. Tankless owners should plan on descaling every year in hard-water areas.

Equipment age matters just as much. With regular service, a standard tank typically lasts 8 to 12 years, a heat pump water heater 10 to 15, and a tankless unit up to 20. Once a heater passes that range and starts acting up, like rust-colored water, popping noises, or moisture under the tank, it's smart to start planning a replacement. Replacing on your own schedule costs less and is far less stressful than an emergency swap with water spreading across the floor.

Left alone, a small problem tends to grow. Consider a spent anode rod: replacing it is a short, inexpensive repair, but if nobody replaces it, the water begins corroding the tank itself, and just like that a minor fix has become a full replacement. That's why we ask homeowners to save our number in their phone and call at the first sign of trouble. Catching it early saves time and money and spares you a flooded utility room.

Hot Water Headaches Gladstone Homeowners Face

In Gladstone, we work on every kind of water heater, and each one has a typical way of failing. Gas tanks tend to have trouble with thermocouples and flame sensors, sooty burners, and draft problems in the flue. Electric units burn out elements and thermostats, especially when sediment buries the lower element. Tankless heaters throw error codes for scale in the heat exchanger, ignition failures, and flow sensor problems, most of which trace back to skipped descaling. Heat pump water heaters need clean air filters and a clear condensate drain, and they don't do well in small, closed-in spaces. Our technicians work on all four every week and carry the parts to fix most problems on the first visit.
