Common Problems

When Homeowners Should Call for Help

Water heater trouble rarely announces itself politely. If you're seeing any of these problems, contact our team right away for fast, professional help.

Water Heater Not Heating

A tripped high-limit switch, a failed gas valve, or a dead igniter can all look like the same symptom. Real diagnosis beats parts-swapping every time.

Fast repair help

Water Around the Heater

Water on the floor can come from a loose fitting, a weeping drain valve, or a relief valve doing its job. Each one means something different, and we sort out which you have.

Leak repair

Popping Noises, Smelly Water

A rotten-egg smell in the hot water usually comes from bacteria reacting with the anode rod. Swapping to a different anode and flushing the tank typically fixes it.

Maintenance & flushing

Old Heater, Repeat Repairs

We size replacements by household demand and first-hour rating, not a guess, so the new heater keeps up with your busiest mornings from day one.

Replacement options

Tankless Error Codes

Hot-cold-hot water, a unit that shuts down mid-shower, or a flashing code usually points to mineral scale. A proper descaling often solves it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Most water heater repairs typically run between $150 and $600, depending on the part. Thermostats, heating elements, thermocouples, and flame sensors sit at the lower end, while a gas control valve can reach $700. If the tank itself is leaking, repair is off the table, and a replacement typically runs $1,100 to $2,800 installed for a standard tank or $2,800 to $5,500 for tankless. We diagnose the problem first and give you a clear, upfront price before any work begins.
If the heater is under about 8 years old and the problem is a part, such as an element, thermostat, thermocouple, or valve, repair usually makes sense. If water is leaking from the tank body, the hot water runs rusty, or a tank unit is past 10 to 12 years old, replacement is the smarter investment, because a corroded tank cannot be patched. Our technicians walk you through both numbers honestly so you can decide with real information.
We offer same-day and emergency water heater service throughout the area.
